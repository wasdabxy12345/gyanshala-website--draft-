Institutional Credibility

External Program Reviews

Gyan Shala subjects its entire organizational model to rigorous external audits. While student assessments prove what children learn, our program reviews evaluate how we deliver high-quality education at a fraction of the cost of traditional private schools.

Brookings Institution: QEI DIB Impact Study

  • Quality Education India Development Impact Bond (QEI DIB), the largest education impact bond, included Gyan Shala alongside three other education providers, running from 2018 to 2022, focused on improving literacy and numeracy for 200,000 students across four states in India.
  • Gyan Shala operated community learning centers in underprivileged urban areas and provided teacher support in pedagogy and learning plans.
  • During school shutdowns, Gyan Shala quickly adopted phone-based instruction, worksheets for students at home, small-group TV lessons in community classrooms, etc., and altered their strategy based on results.
  • The program produced rare high-quality data, which can be used by other educators to improve their learning outcomes. Consequently, the Back-to-School Outcomes Fund, with similar goals for government school students, has learned from and implemented QEI DIB's learning.
